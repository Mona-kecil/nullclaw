NullClaw Personal

A self-hosted assistant that helps its owner manage email and calendars through chat.

Google account access

With the owner's authorization, NullClaw can search and read Gmail, draft and send messages, organize mail, and manage Google Calendar events.

The assistant runs on the owner's computer. It uses local Google connectors and a configured AI model service to carry out authorized tasks. Replies return through the owner's chat channels.

A personal installation

This OAuth app belongs to the personal installation maintained through the Mona-kecil/nullclaw fork. It has no public sign-up service. Other NullClaw installations use their own accounts and settings.

Read the privacy policy for data access, storage, sharing, and deletion. The terms of service describe use of this installation.

For support, contact the maintainer through the fork's issue tracker. Keep account data and credentials out of public issues.
