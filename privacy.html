Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026

This policy covers the NullClaw Personal Google OAuth app and the personal assistant installation maintained through the Mona-kecil/nullclaw fork.

Data access and purpose

The app uses the authorized Google account's identity, Gmail data, and Google Calendar data to carry out the owner's mail and calendar tasks.

Gmail access can include message text, headers, recipients, attachments, labels, and filters. The app can search, read, draft, send, and organize mail. Calendar access can include calendar lists, event details, attendees, and availability. The app can read and manage calendars and events.

Storage and retention

Google OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in private files on the owner's computer. Mail and calendar information returned by tools may remain in local conversation history, memory, logs, or backups.

The owner controls these local records and retains them until they are deleted. Connected AI and chat services handle data under their own terms and retention settings.

Sharing and AI processing

To answer a request, the assistant may send relevant Google data to its configured AI model service. Replies pass through the chat service used for that request. Participants in that chat may see the reply.

Sending an email or inviting an event attendee shares the relevant content with the recipients chosen for the authorized task. Google data is otherwise used or transferred only as needed for the authorized features, security, or legal requirements.

This installation does not sell Google data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or use it to train generalized AI models. It has no routine human review service for Google account data.

NullClaw Personal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revoke access and delete records

The owner can revoke the app's access through Google Account connections and remove the local OAuth credentials. Revocation stops future access. It does not erase prior conversation records or provider records.

To remove retained data, delete the relevant local history, memory, logs, and backups. Use each connected provider's controls to request deletion of records that provider holds.

Contact and changes

Contact the maintainer through the fork's issue tracker. Do not post private account data there.

Changes to data use will be reflected in this policy. New access or purposes require the account owner's consent before use.
